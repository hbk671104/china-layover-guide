# Product Marketing Context — China Layover Guide

- **Document version**: `1.0`
- **Last updated**: `2026-11-27`

## 1. Product Overview

- **One-line description**: Independent, Beijing-tested English guide that helps foreign travelers turn a China layover into a stress-free trip under the 240-hour visa-free transit policy.
- **Website**: `https://chinalayoverguide.com/`
- **Category**: Inbound China travel & transit planning guide (layover logistics, visa-free transit, airline transit hotels, eSIMs, foreign-card payments).
- **Business model**: Content-first free site + free interactive tools, monetizing via contextual travel affiliate partnerships (Travelpayouts: Trip.com for hotels/trains/transfers, GetYourGuide/Klook for layover tours & Great Wall transfers, Airalo/Holafly for travel eSIMs). No display ads or sponsored rankings.

## 2. Target Audience (ICP)

- **Primary travelers**: English-speaking international travelers from **Singapore, US, UK, Australia, New Zealand, Canada, and Western Europe (especially Netherlands/EU)** transiting mainland China hubs (PVG, PEK, PKX, CAN, TFU, XIY, SZX) on Europe↔Asia, Australia↔Europe, or US↔Asia routes.
- **Typical window**: 6 to 72 hours (up to 240 hours / 10 days) on the ground, often on China Eastern, China Southern, or Air China connections.
- **Jobs to be done**:
  1. Confirm whether they can legally leave the airport visa-free (240-hour transit vs. 30-day visa-free vs. visa) and avoid being denied boarding/entry.
  2. Claim a free airline transit hotel (or pick the right airside vs. landside airport hotel if disqualified).
  3. Land connected and able to pay (firewall-bypassing travel eSIM, Alipay/WeChat Pay linked to foreign cards, DiDi ride-hailing).
  4. Decide honestly what actually fits in 6, 8, 10, 12, or 24 hours without risking a missed onward flight.

## 3. Core Problems & Customer Language

- **Real questions travelers ask** (observed in GSC & Reddit):
  - *"Can I leave the airport during a China layover?"*
  - *"Does China Airlines / China Eastern / China Southern / Air China provide a free hotel for a long layover?"*
  - *"Why can't I find the transit hotel option in the China Eastern app?"*
  - *"Do Apple Pay and Google Pay work in China?"*
  - *"Does Google Maps work in China even with an eSIM?"*
  - *"Can you see the Great Wall on a Beijing layover?"*
- **Why alternatives fall short**:
  - Outdated blogs still quoting China's legacy 72/144-hour schemes.
  - Generic travel sites that never tested Chinese apps on foreign passports/cards or never clarify airside vs. landside immigration rules for airport hotels.
  - Airline English portals that redirect, break, or hide Chinese-only booking screens.

## 4. Differentiation & Linkable Assets

- **Beijing-based first-hand editorial team** (Nora Li & Bokang Huang): tests payment apps, airport Wi-Fi, and airline apps directly and cites official NIA / airport / airline sources with visible review dates.
- **Interactive tools**:
  - 240-Hour Visa-Free Transit Eligibility Checker (`/transit-visa/`): 57 nationalities × 65 ports × third-country A→B→C rule.
  - Foreign-Card Payment Recommender (`/payments/`): Alipay / WeChat Pay / TenPayGo setup path.
- **Unique visual & data assets**:
  - Verified 3-step annotated in-app booking guides for **China Eastern (Chinese UI translated field-by-field)**, **China Southern**, and **Air China**.
  - Citable 2026 statistics & policy timeline page (`/guides/china-layover-statistics-and-facts/`).
  - First-hand airport photography (starting with PVG and PKX, expanding to PEK and CAN).

## 5. Brand Voice & Positioning Guardrails

- **Tone**: Calm, direct, honest, anti-hype. Give "the short answer" in the first two sentences. Say plainly when a layover is too short ("Under 6 hours: stay airside") or when an airline's website is broken.
- **Hard writing rules**: No em dashes (`—`), no clickbait ("Ultimate Guide"), no AI tells ("it's not X, it's Y"), no unverified fares or rules.
- **Trust boundary**: Keep pure visa and transit-rule pages completely free of affiliate pitches. Clearly disclose affiliate links on About, Privacy, and commercial hotel/tour/eSIM placements.
