## The short answer

**An 8-hour layover at PVG is enough to see the Bund, if you move deliberately.** You get roughly **2.5–3.5 usable hours** in the city. That is real, but it is not enough for a checklist. Do one thing well and get back early.

This page is about the Pudong side: the Maglev and metro timings, when the last train runs, and the return buffer that keeps you flying. For what to actually see in each window, see [Shanghai layover by window](/guides/shanghai-layover-itinerary/).

## The time budget, honestly

| Step | Time |
|---|---|
| Land, disembark, clear immigration | 45–90 min |
| Store luggage (if needed) | 15–30 min |
| Maglev + Metro Line 2 into the centre | 40–55 min |
| **In the city** | **2.5–3.5 hours** |
| Return to PVG | 40–55 min |
| Check-in, security, exit immigration | 2.5 hours |
| **Total** | **~8 hours** |

Notice how little slack there is. That is not a reason to stay in the terminal. It is a reason to plan.

## The route in

1. **Follow the Maglev signs** from arrivals. It is on the basement level, with a different entrance from the metro.
2. **Buy a ticket**, ¥50, or **¥40 if you show a same-day boarding pass**. The discount is counter-only, so keep your boarding pass handy.
3. **Ride to Longyang Road**: about 8 minutes at up to 300 km/h.
4. **Transfer to Metro Line 2** and continue to Nanjing East Road or People's Square.
5. **Walk to the Bund**, 10–15 minutes from Nanjing East Road.

Total: about 40–55 minutes door to door.

## What to actually do

**The Bund, and one good meal.** That is the honest 8-hour answer.

- Walk the waterfront promenade and look across at the Pudong skyline.
- Eat **xiaolongbao** (soup dumplings) somewhere near Nanjing Road or the old town.
- If you have time left, walk a stretch of **Nanjing Road** on your way back to the metro.

**Do not attempt:** Yu Garden *and* the Bund *and* a sit-down banquet *and* Shanghai Tower. Each one is fine on its own; together they will make you late.

## The return buffer is not negotiable

Be back at PVG **2.5 hours before an international departure**, 3 if you have bags to check. Evening exit immigration plus security regularly stacks 45+ minutes.

A hard constraint to know: **the Maglev stops around 21:40** and **Metro Line 2 around 22:30**. If your layover runs into the evening, your return trip becomes a taxi or DiDi, and you should leave the city earlier.

## Bottom line

Eight hours at PVG buys you the Bund and a proper meal: one of the most photogenic half-days in travel. Take the Maglev, do one thing, and be back with 2.5 hours to spare. For the full airport detail, see the [PVG airport guide](/airports/pvg/).
