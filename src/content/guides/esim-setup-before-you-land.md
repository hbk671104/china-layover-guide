## Why you need this before you land

China's network blocks many apps you use every day — Google services, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, and sometimes Gmail. There are two ways around it:

1. **A travel eSIM** (recommended): your data is routed through a partner network outside China, so blocked apps keep working without a VPN.
2. **A local Chinese SIM**: fast and cheap, but blocked apps stay blocked unless you also set up a VPN.

The catch: **airport Wi-Fi in China usually requires an SMS code**, and your home number may not receive it. Set everything up before departure, not in the arrivals hall.

## Before you buy

- Confirm your phone is **unlocked** and supports **eSIM**.
- Check how much data you'll want: maps, translation, and video calls burn data. 1 GB/day is a comfortable starting point for a layover traveler.
- Check coverage for the cities you'll visit (most plans cover all of mainland China).

## Setup steps, in order

1. **Buy the eSIM at home** and install it while you still have reliable Wi-Fi. Most providers give you a QR code.
2. **Name the plan** in your phone settings (e.g. "China travel") so it's easy to find later.
3. **Turn off your home SIM** or disable data roaming on it to avoid surprise charges.
4. **Enable the travel eSIM** right before landing, or switch it on after you land. Many plans activate on first connection.
5. **Test before you leave the airport**: open Google Maps and WhatsApp while still in the terminal.

## On the ground

- Keep your home SIM off. If your phone asks about "allow data switching", say no.
- Most travel eSIMs are **data-only** — you won't get a Chinese phone number. That's fine: you don't need one for Alipay/WeChat Pay, maps, or ride-hailing.
- If an app asks for SMS verification and you can't receive it, use your home number's roaming SMS if available, or set up the app before you travel.

## Bottom line

A travel eSIM is the highest-leverage thing you can do before a China layover: it keeps your usual apps working and removes the biggest source of airport stress. Buy it early, install it early, and test it before you take off.
