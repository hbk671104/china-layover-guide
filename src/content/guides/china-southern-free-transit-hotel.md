## The short answer

**China Southern is the easiest mainland Chinese airline to actually get a free layover hotel from.** Unlike China Eastern, ordinary economy fares usually qualify (including eligible mileage tickets under one booking code), the **English app works properly**, and **Guangzhou Baiyun Airport (CAN)** has a well-oiled transit hotel counter and shuttle system.

For a side-by-side comparison with Air China, China Eastern, and Taiwan's China Airlines, see our [China airline free transit hotel hub](/guides/china-airline-layover-hotel/). Below is the exact step-by-step playbook for China Southern passengers.

## Eligibility rules & room grades

| Rule | China Southern requirement | Key catch |
|---|---|---|
| **Ticket structure** | Confirmed seats on CSN flights under **the same booking code** | Code-share and ground virtual flights are excluded |
| **Overnight window** | **6–30 hours** (domestic↔international and international↔international) | Domestic leg exclusions apply (for example, international → Guangzhou → Beijing does not qualify) |
| **Same-day window** | Inbound arrives **00:00–08:00** with at least **6 hours** before onward flight | Midday arrivals with an evening flight do not qualify |
| **Long international↔international** | Connections up to **240 hours** are eligible | Covers the **first night only** |
| **What is included** | **1 free night**, complimentary breakfast, and round-trip airport shuttle | Stay length and breakfast hours follow the assigned hotel's rules |
| **Room grades** | **A-grade** (First/Business), **B/C-grade** (Economy / Sky Pearl tier) | Standard economy is assigned **1 room for 2 same-sex passengers**; pay a supplement for a single room |
| **Online booking hubs** | **Guangzhou (CAN), Beijing (PKX), Shenzhen (SZX), Urumqi, Shenyang** | Six other hubs (Dalian, Harbin, Wuhan, Changsha, Zhengzhou, etc.) have narrower rules |

## Step-by-step: how to book in the China Southern English app

Unlike China Eastern, you do not need to wrestle with a Chinese-only menu. China Southern's English app exposes both the booking portal and the full policy:

1. **Open the China Southern app** and tap **`e-Service`** on the bottom navigation bar.
2. Under **Online Service**, tap **`Transfer Accommodation`**.
3. **Sign in or register.** You can log in with your email, Sky Pearl number, or tap **`Overseas mobile phone password login`** if you registered with a non-Chinese number (or tap `Sign Up` at top right).
4. **Select your itinerary and reserve a hotel** from the available tier list for your booking class. Save a screenshot of the confirmation before flying.
5. **Check live policy updates anytime** via **`Me → Travel Guide → Transit Service → Free Accommodations for Transfer Passengers`**.

## Arriving at Guangzhou Baiyun Airport (CAN): step by step

1. **Clear immigration first.** China Southern's free transit hotels are landside. Make sure you qualify for [240-hour visa-free transit](/transit-visa/) or 30-day visa-free entry before booking (see [can you leave the airport during a China layover?](/guides/can-you-leave-the-airport-during-a-china-layover/)).
2. **Walk to the China Southern transit accommodation counter at Gate 50 on the first floor of Terminal 2**, near the domestic arrival exit.
3. **Show your passport, onward boarding pass, and app confirmation.** Staff confirm your hotel voucher and point you to the complimentary shuttle bus (if you forgot to book online, you can still ask at Gate 50, subject to same-day room availability).
4. **Want to explore Guangzhou during your stop?** Once your bags are dropped, Metro Line 3 reaches central Guangzhou in 45–55 minutes for about ¥8. Plan your hours with our [Guangzhou layover hub](/cities/guangzhou/), [Guangzhou 6–24h itinerary](/guides/guangzhou-layover-itinerary/), and [CAN airport guide](/airports/can/).

## What if your ticket does not qualify (or you want your own hotel)?

- **China Southern Pearl Hotel discount:** China Southern's own in-app rules note that non-qualifying passengers can book the **China Southern Airlines Pearl Hotel** at preferential rates (subject to limited daily allocation).
- **Prefer to stay inside T2 airside or 3 minutes from the terminal?** Compare airside **Aerotel Guangzhou (T2)** and landside **Pullman Guangzhou Baiyun Airport** in our [Guangzhou Baiyun Airport (CAN) layover hotels guide](/guides/guangzhou-baiyun-airport-layover-hotels/).
- **Connecting through Beijing Daxing (PKX) or Shenzhen (SZX) instead?** Check our [Beijing airport layover hotels guide](/guides/beijing-airport-layover-hotels/) and [Shenzhen (SZX) airport guide](/airports/szx/).
- **Ground essentials:** set up a travel eSIM before flying ([best eSIM for China](/guides/best-esim-for-china/)) and [DiDi ride-hailing](/guides/didi-and-ride-hailing-in-china/) in case you arrive late after Metro Line 3 closes around 22:30–23:00.
