## The short answer

**Check your airport code first: Beijing Capital (PEK) and Beijing Daxing (PKX) are 80 km apart.** Booking a hotel at the wrong Beijing airport turns a restful overnight into a 2-hour cross-city commute.

- **At PEK (Capital Airport):** if you cannot clear immigration, use the **T3E International Hourly Lounge** airside (opposite Gate E10). If you clear immigration on an overnight layover, **Hilton Beijing Capital Airport** and **Cordis Beijing Capital Airport** are both ~5 minutes from T3 with 24-hour free shuttles.
- **At PKX (Daxing Airport):** **Aerotel Beijing** sits right inside the Northeast Pier (5–10 min walk to check-in), **but it is landside**, so international transit passengers must clear immigration to enter it. Staying strictly airside at PKX limits you to lounges and free rest zones.

## Beijing Capital Airport (PEK): Terminal 3 & Terminal 2

### Staying airside at PEK (no immigration needed)
- **T3E International Hourly Lounge:** located airside inside Terminal 3E international departures, opposite **Boarding Gate E10**. Open 24 hours with small private sleeping rooms billed by the hour, Wi-Fi, light snacks, and hot showers. This is the single cleanest option in Beijing if you do not want to clear immigration.

### Landside at or beside PEK (requires visa or 240h transit)
- **T3-C Public Area Hourly Lounge:** situated on the 2nd floor of the West Arrival Hall inside Terminal 3-C. Ideal for 4–6 hour daytime layovers when you want a bed and shower without leaving the building.
- **Hilton Beijing Capital Airport:** roughly 5 minutes from Terminals 2 and 3 via a complimentary **24-hour shuttle bus**, with full-service dining, a gym, and an indoor pool.
- **Cordis Beijing Capital Airport:** right beside Terminal 3 (~5 minutes by 24-hour free shuttle), known for quiet soundproofing and smooth late-night check-in.

For rail cutoffs, taxi stands, and luggage counters, see our [PEK airport guide](/airports/pek/).

## Beijing Daxing Airport (PKX)

### Staying airside at PKX (no immigration needed)
- International departures on Level 3 has the **Donghai passenger lounge**, plus free terminal rest zones and 24-hour food outlets. Note that Daxing's **Ruqi** and airside **Shimian** hourly rooms sit on the **domestic** airside piers, not the international transit wing.

### Landside inside or near PKX (requires visa or 240h transit)
- **Aerotel Beijing (Northeast Pier, Levels 1–2):** the standout convenience pick at PKX. You walk straight from the terminal (~5 minutes to domestic check-in, ~10 minutes to international check-in) with zero shuttle wait, and rooms are bookable in flexible hourly blocks or overnight. **Critical reminder:** Aerotel Beijing is **landside**, so international transit passengers must hold valid entry eligibility ([240-hour visa-free transit](/transit-visa/) or a visa) to clear immigration and check in.
- **Shimian Hourly Rest Rooms (Landside):** located on Level 1 opposite Gate 16 (west) and Gate 20 (east) for lower-cost short naps.
- **Campus hotels:** Daxing Airport lists **Mumian Hotel (木棉花酒店)** and **Meichen Hotel (美宸酒店)** nearby, alongside properties like Hilton Garden Inn Daxing Airport.

For Daxing Express hours, overnight airside check-in rules (08:00 the day prior until 02:00), and DiDi pickup zones, see our full [PKX airport guide](/airports/pkx/).

## When a downtown Beijing hotel makes more sense

Skip airport hotels if you have **18+ hours**, qualify for visa-free entry, and want to see Beijing rather than stare at terminal walls:
- **Flying out of PEK:** stay near **Dongzhimen, Qianmen, or Wangfujing** so you can walk to sights and catch the 25–35 minute **Capital Airport Express (¥25, runs ~06:00–23:00)** straight back to T2/T3.
- **Flying out of PKX:** stay near **Metro Line 19 or Caoqiao** for a quick switch onto the 20-minute **Daxing Airport Express (¥35, last train from Caoqiao 22:30)**.
- **Planning a morning Great Wall run?** Staying near **Dongzhimen or beside PEK** cuts an hour off your drive to Mutianyu compared with PKX. Read our [Beijing layover hub](/cities/beijing/) and [Great Wall on a Beijing layover guide](/guides/great-wall-from-beijing-layover/).

## Practical arrival checklist

- **Immigration & registration:** every landside hotel requires clearing immigration first (check [can you leave the airport during a China layover?](/guides/can-you-leave-the-airport-during-a-china-layover/)). Licensed Beijing hotels automatically file your mandatory [24-hour foreigner accommodation registration](/guides/hotel-registration-for-foreigners-in-china/) at check-in.
- **Luggage:** PKX's landside manual left-luggage counters are free, while PEK charges roughly ¥30–40 per rolling suitcase per 24 hours. See [luggage storage at China's airports](/guides/luggage-storage-at-china-airports/).
- **Late-night transfers & payments:** both Airport Express lines stop around 22:30–23:00. Set up [DiDi ride-hailing](/guides/didi-and-ride-hailing-in-china/), Alipay / [WeChat Pay](/guides/wechat-pay-and-tenpaygo-for-foreigners/), and a travel eSIM ([best eSIM for China](/guides/best-esim-for-china/)) before you fly, with passport-login [China airport Wi-Fi](/guides/china-airport-wifi/) as backup.
