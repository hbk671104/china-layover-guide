## The short answer

The window you have decides what Shanghai you get. **6 hours is a taste; 8 hours comfortably covers the Bund and a real meal; 12 hours is a full classic day; 24 hours adds a water town or the French Concession.** Usable time is shorter than the layover in every case, because Pudong is about 30 km out.

## Which airport, and how long to reach the city

| Airport | To the center | Cost | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pudong (PVG), Maglev + Metro Line 2 | ~40–55 min | ¥50 + ¥4 (¥40 with same-day boarding pass) | The fastest route in |
| Pudong (PVG), Metro Line 2 direct | 60–90 min | ¥7–9 | Cheapest, or if your hotel is in Pudong |
| Hongqiao (SHA), Metro Lines 2 / 10 | 30–50 min | ¥3–7 | The easiest option by far |

**A hard limit to plan around:** the Maglev stops running around 21:40 and Metro Line 2 around 22:30. Late arrivals are taxi or DiDi territory.

PVG and SHA are about 70 km apart. If your connection uses both airports, treat it as a 1.5–2 hour transfer and do not add sightseeing under 12 hours.

## Getting in from Pudong: the Maglev in detail

1. Follow the **Maglev signs** from arrivals. It is on the basement level, with a separate entrance from the metro.
2. Buy a ticket for **¥50, or ¥40 if you show a same-day boarding pass**. The discount is counter-only, so keep your boarding pass handy.
3. Ride to **Longyang Road**: about 8 minutes at up to 300 km/h.
4. Transfer to **Metro Line 2** and continue to Nanjing East Road or People's Square.
5. Walk to **the Bund**, 10–15 minutes from Nanjing East Road.

## What each window actually gives you

### 6 hours: not worth leaving
By the time you clear immigration (45–90 minutes is normal), ride in, and turn around, there is almost no city time. Stay airside and save the city for a longer stop.

### 8 hours: the Bund and one good meal
Roughly **2.5–3.5 usable hours**. Take the Maglev plus Metro Line 2 to Nanjing East Road, walk to the Bund for the skyline, eat xiaolongbao (soup dumplings), and head back with a 2.5-hour buffer.

Do not attempt Yu Garden, the Bund, a sit-down banquet, and Shanghai Tower. Each is fine on its own; together they will make you late.

### 12 hours: the classic day
About **6 usable hours**. Add Yu Garden and the old town, walk part of Nanjing Road, and cross to Lujiazui for the view back at the Bund. Start with the Bund before the crowds, and finish with dinner.

### 24 hours: a real stopover
Book a hotel in Puxi (People's Square, Jing'an, or the French Concession), see the classic sights unhurried, add a river cruise at night, and use the morning for a day trip: Zhujiajiao water town, or a one-hour high-speed train to Hangzhou.

## The time budget, honestly

| Step | Time |
|---|---|
| Land, disembark, clear immigration | 45–90 min |
| Store luggage (if needed) | 15–30 min |
| Maglev + Metro Line 2 into the center | 40–55 min |
| **In the city** | **2.5–3.5 hours** |
| Return to PVG | 40–55 min |
| Check-in, security, exit immigration | 2.5 hours |
| **Total** | **~8 hours** |

Immigration at PVG is among the slowest of China's big gateways. An evening bank of long-haul arrivals can mean 60–90 minutes, and the visa-free transit lane is slower than the regular queue because officers check your itinerary manually.

## The route that works

1. Land and clear immigration; collect your bags if you have them.
2. Store luggage if you are not checking into a hotel (left-luggage is usually ¥15–30 per bag).
3. Maglev + Metro Line 2 from Pudong, or Metro Lines 2/10 from Hongqiao, into the center.
4. The Bund, then Yu Garden, then Nanjing Road, in that order.
5. Return with 2.5–3 hours before departure if you are checking a bag.

## Shanghai layover tours: are they worth it?

**Worth it if you have 8+ hours and would rather not plan.** A private tour handles the transfer, the route, and the timing, which is exactly the hard part. **Not worth it under 6 hours**, and unnecessary if you are happy taking the Maglev yourself.

A driver meets you in the arrivals hall with a name sign, tracks your flight, and drives you into the city. A guide takes you to a compressed set of highlights (usually the Bund, Yu Garden and the old town, sometimes the French Concession or Shanghai Tower) and gets you back with a buffer. Typical durations are 4–8 hours, structured around your flight times.

| Type | Indicative price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Private tour, airport pickup | ~US$100–220 per person | Varies by group size and inclusions |
| Tour with tickets and lunch | ~US$200–230 | Yu Garden and Shanghai Tower entry often included |
| Shared or group option | Lower | Less flexible on timing |

Prices are indicative and move with season and group size. Check what is **excluded**: many private tours quote vehicle and driver only, with guide service, entrance fees, and meals extra.

**Book one when** you have 8+ hours, you are traveling with family or heavy bags, or timing risk worries you and you would rather the operator carry it.

**Skip it when** you have under 6 hours, or you are comfortable with public transport. The Maglev plus Metro Line 2 costs about ¥54 and takes 40–55 minutes.

**The middle path:** book a private transfer only (car and driver, no guide) and follow the route above yourself. Cheaper than a guided tour, and it still solves the luggage and timing problems.

## Bottom line

Shanghai rewards 8 hours or more. Choose Hongqiao if you can, take the Maglev from Pudong if you cannot, book a tour only if you want the logistics handled, and treat any window under 6 hours as an airport day.
