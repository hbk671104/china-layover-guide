## The short answer

Roughly **three in four** foreign visitors now enter China without a visa: visa-free entries were **77.5%** of foreign entries in the third quarter of 2026. Foreign nationals made **30.08 million** visa-free entries in 2025, up 49.5%, and about **26.8 million** in the first nine months of 2026. The 240-hour transit policy covers **57 countries** through **65 ports** in **24 provinces**, and the 30-day visa-free entry list covers **50 countries**.

Every figure on this page comes from the National Immigration Administration (NIA) or another government source, listed at the foot of the page. We update it after each quarterly NIA release.

## Visa-free entries by foreign nationals

| Period | Visa-free entries | Share of foreign entries | Change, year on year | NIA release |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2025, full year | **30.08 million** | 73.1% | +49.5% | 30 January 2026 |
| Q1 2026 | **8.315 million** | 77.9% | +29.3% | 13 April 2026 |
| First half of 2026 | **17.82 million** | 77.7% | +30.6% | 13 July 2026 |
| Q3 2026 | **8.99 million** | 77.5% | +24.1% | 20 October 2026 |
| January–September 2026 | **about 26.8 million** | — | — | Our sum of the first-half and Q3 figures |

The wider picture from the same releases:

- **2025:** 697 million border crossings in total, a record and up 14.2%, including 82.04 million by foreign nationals, up 26.4%.
- **First half of 2026:** 369 million crossings, up 10.8% and another record. Foreign entries reached 22.91 million, up 20.4%.
- **Q3 2026:** 192 million crossings, up 7.9%. Foreign entries reached 11.60 million, up 15.6%.

These releases count all visa-free entries together. They do not break out the 240-hour transit policy separately from 30-day visa-free entry and the other schemes.

## Visa-free transit (240-hour)

| Fact | Figure |
|---|---|
| Eligible countries | **57** |
| Maximum stay | **240 hours (10 days)** |
| Designated ports | **65** |
| Provinces / regions covered | **24** |
| Clock starts | **00:00 the day after entry** |
| Passport | **Ordinary passport**, valid for at least **3 months** |
| Onward ticket | Confirmed seat and date to a **third country or region** |

## Visa-free entry (30-day)

| Fact | Figure |
|---|---|
| Countries | **50** (35 in Europe, 2 in Oceania, 7 in Asia, 6 in the Americas) |
| Maximum stay | **30 days**, counted from 00:00 the day after entry |
| Purposes | Business, tourism, visiting relatives and friends, exchange visits, transit |
| Third-country requirement | **None** |
| Recent additions | The United Kingdom and Canada are on the list as of 17 February 2026 |
| Notable absence | The United States is **not** on the 30-day list; US citizens can use the 240-hour transit |

## Policy timeline

| Date | Change |
|---|---|
| 1 December 2023 | Unilateral visa-free trial for France, Germany, Italy, the Netherlands, Spain and Malaysia: stays of up to 15 days |
| 30 November 2024 | Visa-free stays extended from 15 to 30 days, exchange visits added as a purpose; 38 countries covered |
| December 2024 | 240-hour visa-free transit replaces the 72- and 144-hour rules: 54 countries, ports up from 39 to 60 in 24 provinces |
| June 2025 | Indonesia added to the transit list (55 countries) |
| 2025 | Five more Guangdong ports bring the transit total to 65 |
| End of 2025 | Unilateral visa-free entry covers 48 countries |
| 17 February 2026 | The NIA's list shows 50 countries for 30-day visa-free entry, including the UK and Canada |
| 20 August 2026 | Kyrgyzstan and Vietnam added to the transit list (57 countries) |

## Key figures to quote

- Foreign nationals made **30.08 million** visa-free entries into China in 2025, **73.1%** of all foreign entries and up **49.5%** on 2024 (NIA, January 2026).
- In the first half of 2026, visa-free entries reached **17.82 million**, **77.7%** of foreign entries, up **30.6%** year on year (NIA, July 2026).
- In the third quarter of 2026, **8.99 million** foreign entries were visa-free, **77.5%** of the total, up **24.1%** (NIA, October 2026).
- China's 240-hour visa-free transit covers **57 countries** through **65 ports** in **24 provinces** (NIA, revised August 2026).
- Ordinary passport holders from **50 countries** can enter China visa-free for up to **30 days** (NIA list, February 2026).
