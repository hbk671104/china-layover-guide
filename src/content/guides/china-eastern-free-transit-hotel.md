## The short answer

**China Eastern gives a free transit hotel (1 night, breakfast, and airport shuttle) on qualifying 6–24 hour layovers, but it is the trickiest mainland carrier to claim.** Two hurdles trip up almost every foreign traveler: the **English app hides the booking button**, and discounted economy fares or non-781 tickets are routinely rejected.

If you are comparing multiple mainland carriers or Taiwan's China Airlines (CI), start with our [China airline free transit hotel comparison](/guides/china-airline-layover-hotel/). If you already hold a China Eastern ticket, follow the exact checklist and Chinese-app walkthrough below.

## Eligibility rules: what actually qualifies

Every requirement below must line up on the same booking:

| Requirement | Rule | What commonly disqualifies people |
|---|---|---|
| **Ticket stock** | 13-digit ticket number starting **`781`** | Tickets issued by a partner airline or codeshare stock |
| **Operating carrier** | China Eastern (`MU`) or Shanghai Airlines (`FM`) on both legs | Partner-operated codeshare legs |
| **Itinerary** | One single through-ticket (one booking reference) | Two separate tickets bought individually, even on China Eastern |
| **Transit window** | **6–24 hours**, taking the earliest available onward connection | Skipping an earlier flight to force an overnight stop |
| **Same-day rule** | Inbound flight must arrive **before 03:00** | A 05:00 arrival connecting to a 14:00 flight the same day |
| **Excluded classes** | Booking classes **A, O, X** and domestic **H** | Cheap promotional fares or award tickets |
| **Fare threshold** | Must clear hub minimum (reported around **RMB 1,800** at Shanghai; lower at Kunming) | Deeply discounted OTA flash fares |
| **Eligible hubs** | **Shanghai (PVG/SHA), Beijing (PKX), Kunming (KMG), Xi'an (XIY)**, plus Nanjing/Wuxi on select routes | Unlisted regional connections |
| **Booking deadline** | Advance booking required (reported cutoff **12 hours** before first flight departure) | Showing up at the airport counter without a prior booking |

## Step-by-step: how to book in the China Eastern app (Chinese UI)

Most travelers hit a dead end because the English version of the China Eastern app omits the transit-hotel screen, and the English desktop form often redirects or errors out. The fastest reliable path is switching the app to Chinese:

1. **Open the China Eastern app and switch to Chinese (`首页`).** Log in to your Eastern Miles account first (create a free account if you do not have one).
2. **Find the search bar at the bottom of the home screen.** Unlike most apps, China Eastern places its search box **near the bottom**, just above the main navigation tabs (you can also tap **`服务大厅`** / Service Hall at the bottom-right of the icon grid).
3. **Search `中转住宿` and tap `中转服务` (Transit Service).**
4. **Fill in the five fields on the `核验客票` (Verify Ticket) form:**
   - **`姓名` (Passenger Name):** for international tickets, enter `SURNAME/GIVENNAME` in capital English letters (for example, `SMITH/JOHN`)
   - **`证件号` (Passport No.):** exact passport number on your reservation
   - **`票号` (Ticket No.):** your 13-digit ticket number starting with `781`
   - **`航班号` (Flight No.):** your inbound or connecting flight code (for example, `MU552`)
   - **`手机号` (Phone):** tap `+86 >` to pick your country code if using a foreign mobile number
5. **Tap the red `核验客票` button.** If your ticket qualifies, the app displays eligible partner hotels and lets you confirm a room.
6. **Screenshot your confirmation immediately.** Tap **`查看我的订单 >`** (View my orders) at the bottom right of the form screen and save the QR code and hotel address offline before flying.

**Fallback channels if the app rejects a valid ticket:** try logging in on desktop at the [China Eastern Overnight Transit Hotel Service page](https://us.ceair.com/en/transit-hotel-application.html), or call **+86-21-20695530** (**95530** inside China). Keep your 781 ticket number ready; phone agents often ask you to retry online first, so do this several days before departure.

## What happens when you land

- **Clear immigration first.** China Eastern's partner hotels sit landside outside security. Verify ahead of time that your passport qualifies for [240-hour visa-free transit](/transit-visa/) or 30-day visa-free entry, and read [can you leave the airport during a China layover?](/guides/can-you-leave-the-airport-during-a-china-layover/).
- **Find the China Eastern transit service counter** in the arrivals hall to confirm your hotel assignment and shuttle bus pickup point.
- **Expect standard airport-perimeter hotels.** Partner hotels are typically 3-to-4-star properties 15 to 30 minutes from the terminal with complimentary breakfast and scheduled shuttle transfers. Solo economy passengers are frequently paired in a twin room unless paying a single-room supplement.
- **Check your hub guide for transport and luggage:** [Shanghai Pudong (PVG)](/airports/pvg/), [Beijing Daxing (PKX)](/airports/pkx/), and [Xi'an (XIY)](/airports/xiy/), plus [luggage storage at China's airports](/guides/luggage-storage-at-china-airports/).

## What if your China Eastern ticket does not qualify?

This is common on promotional economy fares and OTA-split tickets. Do not waste hours fighting the app if your booking class is excluded:

- **Connecting through Shanghai Pudong (PVG)?** Compare airside sleep pods, Aerotel T2, and Da Zhong Airport Hotel in our [Shanghai Pudong Airport (PVG) layover hotels guide](/guides/shanghai-pudong-airport-layover-hotels/).
- **Connecting through Beijing Daxing (PKX) or Capital (PEK)?** See our [Beijing PEK & PKX airport layover hotels guide](/guides/beijing-airport-layover-hotels/) for Aerotel Beijing and terminal rest rooms.
- **Staying airside without entering China?** Stick to airside hourly pods or lounges so you avoid immigration entirely.
- **Before you land:** install a travel eSIM ([best eSIM for China](/guides/best-esim-for-china/)) and link a card to Alipay or [WeChat Pay](/guides/wechat-pay-and-tenpaygo-for-foreigners/) so you can order a [DiDi](/guides/didi-and-ride-hailing-in-china/) if you miss a hotel shuttle.
