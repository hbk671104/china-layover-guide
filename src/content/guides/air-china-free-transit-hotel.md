## The short answer

**Air China offers a free transit hotel (1 night, breakfast, and shuttle where available) under its Transit Accommodation programme across six hubs: Beijing (PEK/PKX), Chengdu (TFU), Shanghai (PVG), Hangzhou, Tianjin, and Wenzhou.** Two rules catch travelers out more than anything else: **on-site airport bookings are never accepted**, and the **Transit Hotel** button is hidden inside **`All Services → Transfer Service`** rather than on the home screen.

If you are comparing multiple mainland carriers or Taiwan's China Airlines (CI), start with our [China airline free transit hotel hub](/guides/china-airline-layover-hotel/). If you hold a `999` Air China ticket, follow the rules and exact English-app steps below.

## Eligibility rules: Standard vs. Deluxe tiers

| Requirement | Standard Transit Accommodation | Deluxe Transit Accommodation |
|---|---|---|
| **Eligible cities / hubs** | **Beijing (PEK, PKX), Chengdu (TFU), Shanghai (PVG), Hangzhou, Tianjin, Wenzhou** | **Beijing (PEK), Chengdu (TFU), Shanghai (PVG)** |
| **How to book** | Air China app, official website, or phone | **Sales hotline `95583` only** |
| **Booking deadline** | At least **24 hours** before inbound arrival (Beijing time) | At least **50 hours** before inbound arrival (Beijing time) |
| **Ticket stock** | 13-digit ticket number starting **`999`** | Same (`999`) |
| **Flight & carrier rule** | `CA` flight numbers; international↔international legs must both be Air China-operated | Same |
| **Transit window** | **6–24 hours** (domestic↔domestic); **6–30 hours** (with an international leg) | Same |
| **Same-day rule** | Inbound flight must arrive **before 08:00** | Same |
| **Excluded fares** | Booking classes **O, I, X, N**, mileage/award tickets, and infants | Same |
| **Walk-up counter booking** | **Not accepted** | **Not accepted** |

**Cross-airport shuttle trap:** Air China notes that where a free hotel shuttle exists, it may only serve one airport. If your itinerary lands at **PEK** and departs from **PKX** (or lands at **PVG** and departs from **SHA**), expect to arrange and pay for your own cross-city transfer via rail or [DiDi ride-hailing](/guides/didi-and-ride-hailing-in-china/).

## Step-by-step: how to find and book in the Air China English app

Many travelers open the Air China app, scan the home screen, and assume the transit hotel is missing. It sits two taps inside the full services directory:

1. **Open the Air China app (English interface) and log in.** On the **Home** screen, scroll to the second row of small service icons and tap **`All Services`** (purple four-square icon below *Reimbursement*).
2. **Select `Transfer Service` in the left-hand menu**, then tap **`Transit Hotel`** (right next to **`Transit Lounge`**).
3. **Review the `Transit Accommodation` product description** (confirm your hub city and Standard vs. Deluxe tier), then tap the red **`Next`** button at the bottom to verify your `999` ticket and reserve a room.
4. **Save your confirmation offline.** Screenshot the confirmed hotel name, address in Chinese, and pickup details before you fly.

**Tip for shorter layovers or airside-only stays:** right beside **Transit Hotel** in Step 2 sits **`Transit Lounge`**. If your Beijing layover is only 4–6 hours or you cannot clear Chinese immigration, check whether your ticket qualifies for Air China's airside transit lounge at PEK Terminal 3E instead of leaving security.

## What happens when you land

- **Clear immigration first.** Air China's partner hotels are landside, and Air China explicitly states that holding valid entry eligibility is the passenger's responsibility. Confirm ahead of time that you qualify for [240-hour visa-free transit](/transit-visa/) or 30-day visa-free entry (see [can you leave the airport during a China layover?](/guides/can-you-leave-the-airport-during-a-china-layover/)).

## What if your Air China ticket does not qualify (or rooms are full)?

Because Air China excludes booking classes `O, I, X, N`, award tickets, and late bookings inside 24 hours, keep a clean backup plan ready:

- **Transiting Beijing (PEK or PKX)?** Compare PEK T3E's airside hourly lounge (no immigration needed), Hilton and Cordis beside T3, and Aerotel Beijing at PKX in our [Beijing PEK & PKX airport layover hotels guide](/guides/beijing-airport-layover-hotels/).
- **Transiting Shanghai Pudong (PVG)?** Compare airside sleep pods, Aerotel T2, and Da Zhong Airport Hotel in our [Shanghai Pudong Airport (PVG) layover hotels guide](/guides/shanghai-pudong-airport-layover-hotels/).
- **Ground essentials:** install a travel eSIM before flying ([best eSIM for China](/guides/best-esim-for-china/)) and link a foreign card to Alipay and [WeChat Pay](/guides/wechat-pay-and-tenpaygo-for-foreigners/) so airport transport and meals work immediately.
