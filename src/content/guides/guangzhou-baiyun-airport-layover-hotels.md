## The short answer

**Guangzhou Baiyun Airport (CAN) is one of the few mainland Chinese hubs with a true airside transit hotel inside the international terminal.** If you are transferring internationally through Terminal 2 and do not want to clear immigration, **Aerotel Guangzhou (T2 Level 4, airside)** sells 6, 9, and 12-hour room blocks right inside security.

If you are clearing immigration on an overnight layover (and do not qualify for **China Southern's free transit hotel**), **Pullman Guangzhou Baiyun Airport** sits right on the airport campus with a 3-minute shuttle to Terminal 2.

*(Flying China Southern? Always check your free-room eligibility first in our [China Southern free transit hotel guide](/guides/china-southern-free-transit-hotel/) before paying out of pocket.)*

## Option 1: Staying airside at Terminal 2 (no immigration needed)

### Aerotel Guangzhou (Terminal 2, Level 4)
- **Location:** inside the **International Departure Secured Area** on Level 4 of Terminal 2.
- **Who can use it:** international transit or departing passengers already holding an onward boarding pass and valid travel documents. Because you stay inside customs and immigration control, you do **not** need a Chinese visa or 240-hour entry permit.
- **Room setup:** compact Solo, Solo Plus, and Double Plus rooms sold in flexible hourly blocks (commonly **6, 9, or 12 hours**), with Wi-Fi and access to the Library Lounge for hot food and drinks.
- **Verify before flying:** Aerotel branches in China occasionally adjust operating hours or undergo temporary refurbishments, so confirm live room availability directly on [Aerotel's official Guangzhou page](https://www.myaerotel.com/en-uk/find/china-east-asia/china/guangzhou/aerotel-guangzhou) before counting on it.

## Option 2: Landside hotels right beside CAN (requires visa or 240h transit)

If you clear immigration under [240-hour visa-free transit](/transit-visa/) or 30-day visa-free entry:

### Pullman Guangzhou Baiyun Airport
- **Where it is:** right on the airport campus (No. 2 Yiheng Road), functioning as CAN's flagship upscale airport hotel.
- **Transfer time:** complimentary scheduled shuttles connect to **Terminal 2 (Gate 46)** in roughly **3 minutes** (plus direct shuttles to other terminals).
- **Why travelers pick it:** over 570 soundproofed rooms, Cantonese and international restaurants, an outdoor pool and gym, and frequent daytime day-use rates for 6–10 hour layovers. Higher-tier Executive bookings often include expedited security-channel perks.

### China Southern Pearl Hotel & perimeter hotels
- If your **China Southern** ticket is excluded from a free transit room (for example, a codeshare or excluded domestic leg), China Southern's in-app transfer policy highlights preferential paid rates at **China Southern Airlines Pearl Hotel** when rooms are available.
- Budget-friendly airport hotels also cluster one to two stops south along **Metro Line 3** (Airport South / Gaozeng), though factor in 15–25 minutes of shuttle or metro time before early departures.

## When staying in central Guangzhou beats an airport hotel

Guangzhou's **Metro Line 3** connects CAN straight to central **Tianhe** in **45–55 minutes for ~¥8**, making a downtown overnight far easier than at Beijing Daxing or Chengdu Tianfu. Choose downtown when:
- Your layover is **14+ hours** and you qualify to [leave the airport](/guides/can-you-leave-the-airport-during-a-china-layover/).
- You want to wake up early for **Cantonese dim sum** (best before 11:00) in **Liwan / Yuexiu**, or stay directly on **Metro Line 3 in Tianhe** for a single-seat return train to CAN.

## Practical arrival checklist

- **Last metro cutoff:** Metro Line 3 stops running around **22:30–23:00**. For late-night landings, take an official taxi or [DiDi ride-hailing](/guides/didi-and-ride-hailing-in-china/) (45–60 min downtown, ¥100–130).
- **Passport registration:** every licensed hotel automatically files your mandatory [foreigner accommodation registration](/guides/hotel-registration-for-foreigners-in-china/) at check-in.
- **Luggage, payments & data:** CAN offers staffed left-luggage counters (~¥15–30 per bag per day; see [luggage storage at China's airports](/guides/luggage-storage-at-china-airports/)). Set up Alipay and [WeChat Pay](/guides/wechat-pay-and-tenpaygo-for-foreigners/) plus a travel eSIM ([best eSIM for China](/guides/best-esim-for-china/)) before you fly, with passport-login [China airport Wi-Fi](/guides/china-airport-wifi/) as backup.
