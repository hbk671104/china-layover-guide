## The short answer

Set up **WeChat Pay as your backup** to Alipay — and set up **WeChat itself** whether or not you pay with it. In China, WeChat is how you book tickets, get hotel messages, and coordinate with drivers. Skipping it leaves you stuck at counters that assume you have it.

## Why you need WeChat, not just Alipay

Alipay is the better wallet for foreign cards. WeChat does things Alipay does not:

- **Mini-programs** — many major attractions, including the Palace Museum (Forbidden City), sell tickets through WeChat mini-programs.
- **Hotel and driver messaging** — check-in details and pickup pins often arrive over WeChat.
- **A second payment rail** — when one app fails at a vendor, the other usually works.

## Set up WeChat Pay

1. **Download WeChat** and register with your own phone number.
2. **Complete real-name verification** with your passport (*Me → Services → Wallet → Real-Name Verification*).
3. **Add a card** (*Me → Services → Wallet → Bank Cards → Add a Card*) and confirm the SMS code from your bank.
4. **Match your details exactly** — the name on the card must match your passport. Virtual and prepaid cards are generally refused.
5. **Test a small payment** at a convenience store.

## Fees and limits

The structure mirrors Alipay: **¥200 or less is normally fee-free; above ¥200 typically adds ~3%**. Foreign-issued UnionPay cards are usually exempt. Spending is capped by your verification level, your bank, and the regulatory ceiling (roughly US$5,000 per transaction, US$50,000 per year). Tencent has periodically offered new-card promotions — read the in-app prompt rather than relying on any blog.

## Where TenPayGo fits in

**TenPayGo** (Tencent's cross-border product) is designed to bridge an international card into the WeChat Pay merchant network — similar in spirit to the older Alipay "Tour Pass" approach. It can be useful if direct card linking has trouble, but availability depends on your region and card, and it is not required. Try direct card binding first; use TenPayGo as a supplement.

## Troubleshooting

- **Payment declined?** Your bank may have flagged China. Call them before you travel and enable foreign transactions.
- **Merchant shows a personal QR?** Foreign cards cannot pay into personal accounts. Ask for the merchant code, switch apps, or use cash.
- **Verification stuck?** Re-do the passport photo somewhere bright; a blurry image or a name mismatch is the usual cause.

## Bottom line

Install both Alipay and WeChat before you fly, verify your passport in each, and link your card. Use Alipay as the wallet and WeChat for bookings and as the backup rail. See [Alipay for foreigners](/guides/alipay-for-foreigners/) and [payments in China](/guides/payments-in-china-for-foreigners/).
