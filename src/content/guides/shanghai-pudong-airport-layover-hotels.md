## The short answer

**There is no full-service hotel airside at Shanghai Pudong Airport (PVG).** Every proper bed at PVG, including **Aerotel Shanghai** inside Terminal 2 and **Da Zhong Airport Hotel** between T1 and T2, sits **landside**, meaning international transit passengers must clear Chinese immigration to check in.

If you cannot clear immigration (or have under 6 hours), stay airside and use PVG's **hourly sleep pods**, lounges, or free 24-hour rest zones and showers. If you can enter China visa-free and have an 8–12 hour overnight stop, **Aerotel T2** (hourly or overnight) and **Da Zhong** (walkable from both terminals) are the two cleanest options.

*(Flying China Eastern or Air China on one through-ticket? First check whether you qualify for a free room in our [China Eastern free transit hotel guide](/guides/china-eastern-free-transit-hotel/) or [China airline free transit hotel hub](/guides/china-airline-layover-hotel/).)*

## Option 1: Staying airside (no immigration required)

If your passport does not qualify for [240-hour visa-free transit](/transit-visa/) or your connection is too short to clear customs twice, stay inside the secured international zone:

- **Hourly sleep pods:** located airside near **Terminal 1 International Departures (Gate 24)** and **Terminal 2 International Departures (Gate 67)**. Each pod has a reclining chair, charging ports, ventilation, and Wi-Fi. Indicative pricing runs around **¥65 per hour** or roughly **¥333 for an overnight block**, though only 10–20 pods sit at each gate so they fill fast during evening waves.
- **Free 24-hour rest zones & showers:** PVG maintains free reclining rest zones (including near T2 Gate C92, Satellite S1 Gate H105, and S2 Gate G148) plus free 24-hour public shower rooms in T1 and T2 international zones. Pack a compact travel towel and toiletries if you plan to use the free showers.

## Option 2: Walkable landside hotels (clear immigration, no shuttle needed)

If you hold a valid visa, 30-day visa-free entry, or qualify for [240-hour visa-free transit](/transit-visa/), clearing immigration opens up real beds right inside the terminal complex:

### Aerotel Shanghai (inside Terminal 2)
- **Where it is:** Levels 6 and 7 inside Terminal 2, landside behind Check-in Counter G.
- **Why travelers pick it:** purpose-built for transits, modern rooms with private showers, and flexible **hourly blocks** alongside standard overnight stays. If you land at 06:00 with a 15:00 onward flight, paying for a 6-hour daytime room beats waiting for a 14:00 hotel check-in.
- **Note for T1 passengers:** T1 and T2 are linked by a covered central walkway (~10 minutes on foot), so Aerotel still works if you fly China Eastern out of T1.

### Da Zhong Airport Hotel (between T1 and T2)
- **Where it is:** directly in the central transport hub connecting Terminal 1 and Terminal 2, right above the Maglev and Metro Line 2 station (split into North and South buildings).
- **Why travelers pick it:** a 5–6 minute indoor walk from both T1 and T2 without ever waiting for a shuttle bus or stepping into rain, making it the default choice for **07:00 departures** or **midnight arrivals**. Rooms are older than Aerotel's, but you get a full-sized hotel bed directly on top of the rail hub.

## Option 3: Airport-campus shuttle hotels

Shanghai Airport Group operates and connects several perimeter hotels including **Ramada Plaza Shanghai Pudong Airport** and **Holiday Inn Express Shanghai Pudong Airport**. They are typically quieter and better value per square meter than Da Zhong, but always check **24-hour shuttle schedules** and pickup gate numbers before landing after 23:00.

## When a downtown Puxi hotel beats sleeping at PVG

Skip airport hotels entirely when **all three** are true:
1. Your layover is **14 hours or longer** (and you qualify to [leave the airport](/guides/can-you-leave-the-airport-during-a-china-layover/)).
2. You land **before 20:00** so you can still catch the **Maglev (last train 21:42)** or **Metro Line 2 (last train ~22:30)** without paying night-rate taxi fares (~¥218+ into downtown).
3. Your onward flight departs **after 10:30 the next morning**.

Book near **People's Square, Nanjing East Road, or Jing'an** directly on **Metro Line 2** so you can see the Bund at night and ride one direct rail line back to PVG. ## Practical checklist before checking in

- **Passport registration:** licensed Chinese hotels automatically handle mandatory [foreigner accommodation registration](/guides/hotel-registration-for-foreigners-in-china/) when they scan your passport at check-in.
- **Late-night transport:** if you miss the last Maglev/Metro, official taxis wait outside T1 Gate 12 and T2 Gate 25, and [DiDi ride-hailing](/guides/didi-and-ride-hailing-in-china/) picks up on **level B2 in P2 car park**.
- **Bags & connectivity:** store heavy luggage at PVG's left-luggage counters ([luggage storage at China's airports](/guides/luggage-storage-at-china-airports/)) if you only want an overnight pack in town, and set up an eSIM ([best eSIM for China](/guides/best-esim-for-china/)) and Alipay / [WeChat Pay](/guides/wechat-pay-and-tenpaygo-for-foreigners/) before you fly.
