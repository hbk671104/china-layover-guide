## The short answer

Beijing is the classic first stop in China, but it is **not the easiest layover**: the airports are far out and the sights are spread apart. With **8 hours you get one sight done properly**; with **12 hours, a real Beijing day**; with **24 hours or more, the Great Wall becomes possible**.

## Airport to city: the numbers

| Airport | To the city | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Capital (PEK), Airport Express | 25–35 min to Dongzhimen / Sanyuanqiao | ¥25 | Runs ~06:00–23:00, every ~10 min |
| Daxing (PKX), Daxing Airport Express | 30–40 min to Caoqiao (Line 10) | ¥35 | Then Metro Line 10/14/16 onward |
| Taxi / DiDi | 40–70 min | ¥100–200 | Best for late arrivals or heavy bags |

Budget 45–90 minutes for immigration on arrival, more if a bank of long-haul flights lands at once. On the way out, be back at the airport **2.5 hours before an international departure**.

## What each window actually gives you

### 6 hours, stay in the terminal
Immigration, the transfer, and the return buffer consume almost everything. Not worth the stress.

### 8 hours: one sight, done properly
About **2.5–3 hours** on the ground. The realistic pick is **Tiananmen Square and a hutong walk**, or the **Temple of Heaven**. Pick one; do not try to chain sights across the city.

### 12 hours: a real Beijing day
About **5–6 usable hours**. Do the **Forbidden City** (pre-booked) plus **Jingshan Park** for the view over the palace roofs, and a Peking duck lunch. This is the sweet spot for a first visit.

### 24 hours+: add the Great Wall
Overnight in the city and use the morning for **Mutianyu**, roughly 1.5–2 hours each way by pre-booked car. It is quieter than Badaling and has a toboggan down. Keep the city sights for the evening before.

## The route that works

1. Clear immigration and store any bags you are not taking (left-luggage, usually ¥15–30 per bag).
2. Airport Express to Dongzhimen, then Metro or a taxi onward.
3. Forbidden City from the south (Tiananmen) gate → exit north → Jingshan Park.
4. Walk a hutong near the Drum Tower or Nanluoguxiang if time allows.
5. Return with a 2.5-hour buffer; the Airport Express is the most predictable option.

## Beijing-specific traps

- **Forbidden City tickets are passport-linked and sell out.** Book online before you travel.
- **PEK and PKX are on opposite sides of the city**, roughly 80 km apart. A cross-airport connection is 1.5–2 hours by road: never combine it with sightseeing.
- **The Airport Express stops running just after 23:00.** Late arrivals need a taxi or DiDi.
- **Winter air can be harsh** and summer is hot; build in indoor stops.

## Bottom line

Beijing needs 12 hours to feel worth it and 24 to include the Wall. Pre-book the Forbidden City, use the Airport Express, and never book a cross-airport connection you also plan to sightsee on.
