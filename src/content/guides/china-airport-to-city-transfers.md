## The short answer

Every major Chinese airport connects to its city by **metro or a dedicated express train**, usually for under ¥35. **The metro is almost always the right default**; taxis and DiDi are for late arrivals, heavy bags, or groups. Budget **60–90 minutes** door to door and be back **2.5 hours** before an international departure.

## The numbers, airport by airport

| Shanghai Pudong (PVG) | Metro Line 2 direct | 60–90 min | ¥7–9 |
| Shanghai Hongqiao (SHA) | Metro Lines 2 / 10 | 30–50 min | ¥3–7 |

| Hangzhou Xiaoshan (HGH) | Metro / Airport bus | ~45–60 min | ~¥20–30 |

Treat fares and journey times as indicative. They change, so check the airport's own transport page or a maps app on arrival.

## How to choose

- **Daytime, light luggage:** metro or express train. Cheapest and traffic-proof.
- **Heavy bags or a group:** taxi or DiDi, especially from Pudong.
- **Late arrival:** taxi or DiDi only; the metro has stopped.
- **A cross-airport connection** (for example Shanghai PVG to SHA, or Beijing PEK to PKX): allow **1.5–2 hours** and do not also plan sightseeing.

## Paying for the ride

- **Metro and Maglev:** buy a single ticket with cash, or use Alipay/WeChat Pay QR at the gates. Shanghai gates also accept contactless foreign cards.
- **Taxi:** metered; pay with cash or a QR app. Most drivers do not take foreign cards.
- **DiDi:** China's ride-hailing app. Fares are shown upfront and international cards are supported. You need a data connection, see [eSIM setup](/guides/esim-setup-before-you-land/).

## Rules that save you money and trouble

- **Use the official taxi queue**, never a driver soliciting inside arrivals.
- **Have your destination in Chinese characters** on your phone.
- **Avoid airport currency exchange**; use an ATM at a major bank instead (see [do you need cash in China?](/guides/do-you-need-cash-in-china/)).
- **Check the last train time** before you count on the metro.

## Bottom line

China's airport transfers are cheap, fast, and well signed in English. Pick the metro unless you land late or carry a lot, and always check the last service time before you rely on it. Next, see [what you can do in each layover window](/cities/).
