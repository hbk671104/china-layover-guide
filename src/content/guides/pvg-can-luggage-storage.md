## The short answer

Both **PVG and CAN have left-luggage counters (行李寄存)**, typically **¥15–30 per bag per day**. Bring your passport, keep the receipt, and you are free to explore. But if you are taking a **private car or DiDi**, the best storage is the boot of the car — the driver waits and you skip every counter.

## The options, ranked

| Option | Cost | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Keep bags in your DiDi / private car** | Free (in the fare) | Most layovers with a car |
| **Airport left-luggage** | ¥15–30 per bag per day | Public-transport layovers |
| **Hotel storage** | Free if you are a guest | Overnight stopovers |
| **Carry a small backpack only** | Free | Short layovers, fastest |

## PVG (Shanghai Pudong)

- **Where:** left-luggage counters in the arrivals and departures halls.
- **Cost:** around ¥15–30 per bag per day — indicative; check the counter's own board.
- **What you need:** passport, and sometimes your onward ticket.
- **Watch out:** the Maglev stops around 21:40, so collect your bags with time to spare if you are returning late.

## CAN (Guangzhou Baiyun)

- **Where:** left-luggage counters in the terminal.
- **Cost:** around ¥15–30 per bag per day — indicative.
- **What you need:** passport.
- **Watch out:** you are taking Metro Line 3 back (45–55 minutes), so allow for that plus your 2.5-hour departure buffer.

## The option most travelers miss

If you are hiring a **private driver or taking DiDi for the day** — common for the [Great Wall](/guides/great-wall-from-beijing-layover/) or the [Terracotta Army](/guides/xian-layover-itinerary/) — the driver waits with the car. Your bags stay in the vehicle, you skip storage entirely, and you save 20–30 minutes at each end.

## What to keep with you

Never store these, at any airport:

- **Passport** and travel documents
- **Boarding pass and onward ticket details** — you may need to show them at immigration
- **Phone, chargers, power bank**
- **Medication**
- **Payment cards and cash**

## Practical tips

- **Photograph your bag** and note the tag number.
- **Pack valuables in a carry-on** you keep with you.
- **Never leave bags unattended** — not in terminals, not in cafés.
- **Check the counter's closing time.** Not all are 24-hour.
- **Collect early** before an evening departure bank, when queues form.

## Bottom line

Luggage should never stop you leaving the airport. Use a car if you have one, a counter if you do not, and always keep documents and valuables on your person. For the general picture, see [luggage storage at China's airports](/guides/luggage-storage-at-china-airports/).
