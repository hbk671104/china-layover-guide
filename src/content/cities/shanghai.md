## Why Shanghai works for a layover

Shanghai is the easiest big-city layover in China: two airports, a Maglev train, and the country's most international downtown. If you only have one day, this route gives you the classic skyline and old-town contrast without rushing.

## The route

Start on the **Bund** for the skyline, walk 15 minutes south to **Yu Garden** for the old-town feel and a quick bite, then cut through **Nanjing Road** on your way back to Metro Line 2. If you have extra time, add a museum or the French Concession.

## Food to try

Soup dumplings (*xiaolongbao*), scallion oil noodles, and a quick *jianbing* from a street stall. Pay with Alipay/WeChat Pay — cash is increasingly rare.

## Timing notes

Pudong Airport is far from the center; budget at least 90 minutes each way. Aim for a layover of 8+ hours to make the trip worthwhile, and always leave a buffer for immigration queues.
