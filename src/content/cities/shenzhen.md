## Why Shenzhen works for a layover

Shenzhen is China's tech capital and one of the fastest, most modern cities in the country. It sits directly on the Hong Kong border, which makes it a natural layover for travelers routing between mainland China and Hong Kong. The airport is a genuine international hub, and the metro gets you into the thick of it quickly.

## The route

Take **Metro Line 11** from Bao'an Airport toward Futian. Start at the **Ping An Finance Centre** for the observation deck and the skyline, then head to **OCT Loft**, a converted factory district full of galleries, cafés, and design shops. Finish at **Shenzhen Bay Park** for the waterfront, or **Dongmen** for street food and shopping, before returning on Line 11.

## Food to try

Cantonese-style dim sum (Shenzhen sits in Guangdong), freshly made rice noodles, and the city's huge range of regional Chinese restaurants: the tech workforce means almost every province is represented.

## Timing notes

The airport is close by the standards of Chinese hubs: Line 11 reaches the central districts in around 30–50 minutes. Still, allow a 2.5-hour buffer before an international departure and check the last metro time. With under 6 hours, stay at the airport.

## Border note

Shenzhen has land crossings to Hong Kong (Futian, Luohu, and Shenzhen Bay). If your itinerary enters or leaves through Hong Kong, keep your onward documents handy, see [can you leave the airport during a layover?](/guides/can-you-leave-the-airport-during-a-china-layover/).
