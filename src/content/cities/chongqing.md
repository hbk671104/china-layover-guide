## Why Chongqing works for a layover

Chongqing is the city that breaks the internet: monorails threading through apartment blocks, a riverside skyline stacked up hillsides, and a night view that looks like a film set. It is also the hotpot capital of China. If your route passes through western China, it is one of the most memorable short stops you can make.

## The route

Take **Metro Line 10** from Jiangbei Airport to the **Jiefangbei** area. Start at the **Liziba** viewpoint, where Line 2 runs straight through a residential building. Ride the **Yangtze River Cableway** across the river, explore the steep stairway neighborhoods, then finish at **Hongyadong**: an 11-storey stilted complex best seen illuminated at night. If you have more time, **Ciqikou** ancient town is a half-day add-on.

## Food to try

Chongqing hotpot with the nine-grid pot, chuan chuan skewers, xiaomian (spicy noodles), and suanlafen (hot-and-sour sweet potato noodles). Ask for mild if you are unsure. The default is genuinely spicy.

## Timing notes

Line 10 reaches the center in 40–50 minutes, so 8 hours is workable and 12 is comfortable. Budget extra time for the hills and traffic if you take a taxi, and be back at the airport 2.5 hours before an international departure.

## Combine with Chengdu

Chongqing and Chengdu are about 1.5 hours apart by high-speed rail, so longer transit stops can pair the two. See the [Chengdu layover guide](/cities/chengdu/) for the other half.
