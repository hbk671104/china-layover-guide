## Why Guilin works for a layover

Guilin is the landscape on the back of the twenty-yuan note: limestone karst peaks rising out of flat river plains. It is one of the most photographed places in China and, for travelers on a longer transit stop, one of the most rewarding.

## The route

In the city itself, start at **Elephant Trunk Hill**, then visit the **Reed Flute Cave** with its illuminated limestone formations. In the evening, the **Two Rivers and Four Lakes** area is lit up and pleasant to walk. With a full day or an overnight, add the **Li River cruise** to Yangshuo — the signature experience, and a morning departure.

## Food to try

Guilin rice noodles (*mifen*) — the local staple, cheap and excellent — plus beer fish (a Yangshuo specialty) and stuffed river snails.

## Timing notes

The Li River cruise is a half-day commitment with a morning departure, so it does not fit a short layover. With 10+ hours, stick to the city sights and the evening lakes. With 24+ hours, overnight in Guilin or Yangshuo and do the river properly.
