## Why Xi'an works for a layover

Xi'an is the shortest practical jump from an international airport to one of the ancient world's great archaeological sites: the **Terracotta Army**. Even if your layover is too tight for the warriors, central Xi'an packs its highlights inside one compact historic square: the intact **Ming-era City Wall**, the illuminated **Bell and Drum Towers**, and the **Muslim Quarter** street-food alleys.

Because **China Eastern** uses Xi'an as a major western hub alongside international routes across Asia and Europe, Xi'an frequently appears on affordable long-layover itineraries. Knowing whether you have enough hours for the Terracotta Army or should stay inside the City Wall is the key decision.

## Arrival airport & transfer math

International flights arrive at **[Xi'an Xianyang International Airport (XIY)](/airports/xiy/)**, roughly 40 km northwest of the city center:

- **Metro Line 14 (~¥7):** connects XIY into Xi'an's metro network, traffic-proof and inexpensive, though reaching the South Gate or Bell Tower requires a line transfer.
- **Airport Bus Line 1 (~¥25, 40–50 minutes):** direct to the railway station and key central stops.
- **Taxi or DiDi (40–60 minutes, ¥100–130):** the fastest option to downtown, and the only sensible way to go straight from XIY to the Terracotta Army (roughly an hour's drive from the airport/city). Set up [DiDi ride-hailing](/guides/didi-and-ride-hailing-in-china/) before flying, or pre-book a round-trip driver if visiting the warriors.

## Planning by layover length

- **Under 6 hours:** stay airside. Once immigration, transfers, and the 2.5-hour international return buffer are deducted, there is not enough margin even for the City Wall.
- **8 hours (city only):** skip the Terracotta Army and head into town for the **Xi'an City Wall** at South Gate (walk or rent a bike along the ramparts) plus lunch in the **Muslim Quarter**.
- **10–12 hours (Terracotta Army focus):** go straight to the **Terracotta Warriors** with a pre-booked round-trip driver, spend 2–3 hours across Pits 1–3, and return directly to XIY. See our detailed [Xi'an 6, 8, 12, and 24-hour layover itinerary](/guides/xian-layover-itinerary/) for exact timing math.
- **24 hours+:** do the Terracotta Army early in the morning, spend the afternoon cycling the City Wall, eat through the Muslim Quarter at dusk, and see the lit-up Bell and Drum Towers at night.

## Where to stay: airline transit hotel vs. inside the City Wall

If you are flying **China Eastern** (ticket prefix 781) on an eligible overnight through-ticket through Xi'an, check whether your fare qualifies for a complimentary transit hotel before paying out of pocket. Full eligibility rules and application steps are in our [China airline free transit hotel guide](/guides/china-airline-layover-hotel/).

If you are booking your own hotel:

- **Late-night arrival or early departure:** stay beside XIY to avoid an hour-long transfer in the dark.
- **16–24 hour layover:** stay inside or just south of the **City Wall (Bell Tower / South Gate area)** so you can walk to the ramparts and Muslim Quarter without wasting transit time. Hotels complete mandatory [foreigner accommodation registration](/guides/hotel-registration-for-foreigners-in-china/) automatically at check-in.

## Practical logistics on the ground

- **Entry rules:** XIY is a designated port under China's [240-hour visa-free transit policy](/transit-visa/), covering Shaanxi province and cross-province permitted areas. Double-check your eligibility in [can you leave the airport during a China layover?](/guides/can-you-leave-the-airport-during-a-china-layover/).
- **Luggage:** XIY offers staffed left-luggage counters (~¥15–30 per bag per day), or leave bags in the trunk if you hire a private driver for the Terracotta Army. See [luggage storage at China's airports](/guides/luggage-storage-at-china-airports/).
- **Payments and connectivity:** Muslim Quarter vendors and bike rentals are QR-only. Set up Alipay and [WeChat Pay](/guides/wechat-pay-and-tenpaygo-for-foreigners/) on a foreign card before flying, and install a travel eSIM ([best eSIM for China](/guides/best-esim-for-china/)) before landing.

## Food and local traps

Do not miss **roujiamo** (spiced braised meat in crispy flatbread), **biangbiang noodles** (wide hand-pulled chili oil noodles), lamb skewers, and **yangrou paomo** (lamb broth with hand-crumbled flatbread). Two traps ruin Xi'an layovers: **Terracotta Army tickets are passport-linked and sold in timed waves** (book ahead rather than showing up at the gate), and catching an independent return taxi from the Terracotta Army back to XIY on a tight schedule is unreliable, so lock in a waiting round-trip driver.
