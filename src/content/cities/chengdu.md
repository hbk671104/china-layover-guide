## Why Chengdu works for a layover

Chengdu is western China's standout layover city: giant pandas in the morning, slow bamboo-chair teahouses in the afternoon, and Sichuan food that rewards every hour you spend outside the terminal. With **Sichuan Airlines** and **Air China** operating growing international routes through Chengdu, more Europe-to-Asia and Australia-to-Europe travelers are getting long connections here.

The main constraint is geography. Most international flights now use **Chengdu Tianfu International Airport (TFU)**, roughly 50 km southeast of the city center, while the **Chengdu Research Base of Giant Panda Breeding** sits on the northeast edge of town. Good timing turns a Chengdu layover into a highlight; landing in the afternoon means skipping pandas and focusing on tea, alleys, and food instead.

## Arrival airport & transfer math

Almost all international transits arrive at **[Chengdu Tianfu International Airport (TFU)](/airports/tfu/)**:

- **Metro Line 18 (express rail):** reaches the city rail network in **35–40 minutes** for **¥7–10**, traffic-proof and modern, though reaching central sights or the Panda Base still requires an interchange.
- **Taxi or DiDi:** **50–70 minutes** and **¥120–180**, often the smartest move on a tight morning connection if you are heading straight from TFU to the Panda Base with luggage stored in the boot. Set up [DiDi ride-hailing](/guides/didi-and-ride-hailing-in-china/) before landing.
- **Dual-airport check:** domestic connections sometimes use older **Chengdu Shuangliu Airport (CTU)** on the southwest side of town. Confirm both legs use TFU before planning a short layover trip.

## Planning by layover window

- **Under 7 hours:** stay airside. Tianfu's distance plus immigration and the 2.5-hour international return buffer leave no comfortable city window.
- **8–9 hours (morning arrival only):** go directly to the **Chengdu Panda Base**. Giant pandas are most active during morning feeding before **10:00** and sleep through much of the afternoon.
- **10–12 hours:** combine a morning Panda Base visit with an hour sipping jasmine tea at **People's Park** (watch locals play mahjong) and a snack walk through **Jinli Street** or **Kuanzhai Alley**. See our full [Chengdu 6, 8, 12, and 24-hour layover itinerary](/guides/chengdu-layover-itinerary/) for exact route timing.
- **24 hours+:** stay overnight in central Chengdu, add a full Sichuan hotpot dinner, and catch a traditional Sichuan opera face-changing show before heading back to TFU.

## Where to stay: Sichuan Airlines / Air China free hotel vs. downtown

Chengdu has one of the most generous free transit hotel setups in China:

- **Sichuan Airlines** (ticket prefix **876**) provides free transit hotels for connections of **6–48 hours domestic** and up to **6–240 hours** when an international leg is included, covering both TFU and CTU. Book in advance (30 days out until 4 hours before departure).
- **Air China** (ticket prefix **999**) also covers TFU under its Transit Accommodation rules, requiring advance booking (24 hours for Standard, 50 hours for Deluxe). Check exact terms in our [China airline free transit hotel guide](/guides/china-airline-layover-hotel/).

If you book your own room: stay near **TFU** for a short overnight under 12 hours, or downtown around **Chunxi Road / Taikoo Li / People's Park** for a 16–24 hour layover so you can walk straight to teahouses and restaurants. Hotels handle mandatory [foreigner accommodation registration](/guides/hotel-registration-for-foreigners-in-china/) automatically.

## Practical logistics on the ground

- **Entry rules:** TFU is a designated port under China's [240-hour visa-free transit policy](/transit-visa/), covering 11 permitted cities in Sichuan (including Chengdu and Leshan) plus cross-province permitted areas. Confirm your entry route in [can you leave the airport during a China layover?](/guides/can-you-leave-the-airport-during-a-china-layover/).
- **Luggage:** TFU has staffed left-luggage counters (~¥15–30 per bag per day). See [luggage storage at China's airports](/guides/luggage-storage-at-china-airports/) for tips.
- **Payments and connectivity:** link a foreign card to both Alipay and [WeChat Pay](/guides/wechat-pay-and-tenpaygo-for-foreigners/) before departure, and activate a travel eSIM ([best eSIM for China](/guides/best-esim-for-china/)) so navigation works from the moment you step off the plane.

## Food and local traps

Try **dan dan noodles**, **mapo tofu**, **zhong dumplings**, and **Sichuan hotpot** (ask for *wei la*, mild spice, if you are not used to numbing Sichuan peppercorns). Two traps to avoid: **Panda Base tickets are passport-linked and busy**, so book online ahead of arrival, and do not waste a 3pm arrival driving out to see sleeping pandas when People's Park and Kuanzhai Alley are far better afternoon stops.
